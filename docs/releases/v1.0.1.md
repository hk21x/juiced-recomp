Fixes for the unofficial native PC port of Juiced (original Xbox, USA/Europe disc).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Updating

Already playing v1.0.0? Open **JuicedLauncher.exe**: it offers this version's launcher, then gets the game ready
again. New players: download **JuicedLauncher.exe** below, put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for
example `C:\Games\Juiced`) and run it. See
[Getting started](https://github.com/hk21x/juiced-recomp#getting-started).

## Fixed

- **The results screen and the replay were very slow at high render scales** (about 5 fps at 200%). The text and
  pictures drawn over them copied the whole picture each time; they no longer do.
  ([#3](https://github.com/hk21x/juiced-recomp/issues/3))
- **Cars in the replay looked very blurry in some camera views**, drawn several times side by side. The replay's
  depth of field (and the race's speed blur) now blur as on the console.
  ([#2](https://github.com/hk21x/juiced-recomp/issues/2))
- **The race map was zoomed out and squashed on wide screens**, its car markers tall ovals reaching past the ring. It
  now looks as it does at 4:3 on every screen shape, as do the cars in the menus' panels.
- **If the graphics card stops responding** (Windows resets it), the game now says so and closes, with what to try,
  instead of freezing on a black or still picture.

## Known issues

- Experimental high FPS can judder or misdraw in some races.
- Not yet covered by testing: a long career, split screen, wet races and NVIDIA graphics cards (tested on AMD and Intel
  Arc).
- The menus' blurred sides are Direct3D 12 only; Direct3D 11 shows black bars there.

## Files

- **JuicedLauncher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **Juiced-package.zip** and **Juiced-package.zip.json**: the package the launcher downloads, and its size and
  checksum. You do not need to download or unzip them.
