The first release of the unofficial native PC port of Juiced (original Xbox, USA/Europe disc).

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: an `.iso` of your disc, or a folder with its
files. The game's code is built on your PC from your disc.

## Installing

Download **JuicedLauncher.exe** below, put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
`C:\Games\Juiced`) and run it. Choose your disc image or folder, let it get the game ready, then press **Play**. See
[Getting started](https://github.com/hk21x/juiced-recomp#getting-started).

## What's in it

- **Widescreen and ultrawide:** a true wider view in races, the menus kept in their 4:3 shape with blurred sides, and
  the race HUD at the screen's sides or in the centre (or hidden: **F9** in a race).
- **Higher frame rates:** the game keeps its own 60 steps a second, and **Smooth motion** draws frames between them up
  to your frame cap.
- **Sharper pictures:** render scale up to 200%, MSAA, FXAA, SMAA, sharpening and anisotropic filtering.
- **PC SETTINGS** in the game's own Options menu (and in the pause menu), and the **F1** settings window.
- **Keyboard and mouse:** keyboard driving with on-screen key prompts, a mouse pointer in the menus, controller
  buttons shown as modern pictures.
- **Steering wheels** with force feedback, and **achievements**.
- **Skip intro movies**, Alt+Enter for full screen, F12 for screenshots.

## Known issues

- Neon flicker in Showoff has been seen with Smooth motion above 60: turn Smooth motion off if you see it.
- Not yet covered by testing: a long career, split screen and wet races.
- The menus' blurred sides are Direct3D 12 only; Direct3D 11 shows black bars there.

## Files

- **JuicedLauncher.exe**: the only file to download by hand. It fetches the rest itself.
- **Juiced-package.zip** and **Juiced-package.zip.json**: the package the launcher downloads, and its size and
  checksum. You do not need to download or unzip them.
